# 啵啵几何工坊隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 6 日  
最近更新：2026 年 9 月 7 日

## 一、适用范围与运营者

本隐私政策适用于“啵啵几何工坊”iOS 应用（以下简称“本应用”）。本应用运营者为 App Store 产品页面所示的开发者或销售商（以下简称“我们”）。请在使用本应用前阅读本政策，了解相关数据处理方式。

## 二、我们处理的信息

1. **计算输入、计算结果和计算历史**：你输入的边长、角度及场景尺寸会在设备上用于完成计算和绘图。保存到历史记录的内容最多保留 20 条，存储于本应用的本地沙盒中。我们不会将这些内容上传到开发者服务器。
2. **应用偏好**：语言、字体大小、场景开关和工具顺序等设置保存在设备上，用于维持你的使用偏好。
3. **订阅权益**：本应用使用 Apple StoreKit 获取订阅产品信息，并核验当前 Apple 账户是否具有有效订阅权益。我们不接触你的付款卡号、Apple 账户密码或完整付款资料，也不在开发者服务器保存交易记录。

## 三、我们不收集的信息

当前版本：

- 不要求注册账户；
- 不包含开发者运营的后台服务；
- 不包含广告、用户追踪、行为埋点或第三方分析 SDK；
- 不请求相机、照片、麦克风、通讯录或位置权限；
- 不出售个人信息，也不将本应用内的数据用于定向广告。

## 四、复制、分享与第三方服务

只有在你主动点击“复制结果”时，相关计算内容才会写入系统剪贴板。

只有在你主动使用分享功能并选择接收应用后，相关计算内容才会交给该应用。接收方如何处理相关信息，受其自身隐私政策约束。

购买、续订、退款、家庭共享、订阅恢复，以及 Apple 提供的可选诊断或 App 分析由 Apple 独立处理，并受 Apple 的条款和隐私政策约束。我们不对 Apple 独立收集的数据作控制。你可以阅读 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) 了解详情。

## 五、信息保存与删除

- 计算历史保存在设备上，直至你逐条删除、使用“清空历史”或卸载本应用。
- 偏好设置保留至你修改、恢复默认设置或卸载本应用。
- 未保存的输入通常随页面或应用状态重置而清除。
- Apple 保存的购买和订阅记录由 Apple 依据其政策管理。你可以在系统“设置”或 App Store 的 Apple 账户订阅页面管理或取消订阅。

## 六、你的选择与权利

你可以随时选择不保存计算历史、删除或清空本地历史、更改偏好设置、停止使用或卸载本应用。

由于当前版本不在开发者服务器保存账户或计算数据，我们通常没有可供访问、更正或删除的服务器端个人数据。如你对本应用的数据处理方式有疑问，可以通过本政策第十条所列渠道联系我们。

## 七、信息安全

本地信息受 iOS 应用沙盒及设备安全机制保护。任何存储方式都无法保证绝对安全，请妥善保护你的设备和 Apple 账户。

## 八、儿童隐私

本应用是通用计算工具，不以儿童为主要对象，也不会主动收集儿童个人信息。如果未来的数据处理方式发生变化，我们将先更新本政策，并遵守适用的儿童隐私要求。

## 九、跨境处理与政策更新

我们不运营用于接收本应用数据的服务器，因此不会主动将你的计算数据传输至境外。Apple 或你选择的分享接收方可能在不同国家或地区处理信息，具体以其隐私政策为准。

如果本应用的功能或数据处理方式发生实质变化，我们会更新本政策及生效日期，并通过应用内页面或 App Store 隐私政策网址提供新版内容。

## 十、联系我们

如对本政策或本应用的数据处理方式有疑问，请通过 App Store 产品页面所列的“App 支持”链接联系开发者。

# Bobo Geometry Workshop Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026  
Last updated: September 7, 2026

## 1. Scope and Operator

This Privacy Policy applies to the Bobo Geometry Workshop iOS app (the “App”). The operator is the developer or seller identified on the App Store product page (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Please read this Policy before using the App to understand its data practices.

## 2. Information We Process

1. **Calculation inputs, results, and history:** Measurements and angles you enter are processed on your device to calculate and draw results. Up to 20 saved history records are stored in the App's local sandbox. We do not upload this content to a developer server.
2. **App preferences:** Language, text size, tool visibility, and tool order are stored on your device to preserve your preferences.
3. **Subscription entitlement:** The App uses Apple StoreKit to load subscription product information and verify whether the current Apple Account has an active entitlement. We do not receive payment card numbers, Apple Account passwords, or complete payment details, and we do not store transaction records on a developer server.

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

The current version:

- requires no user account;
- includes no developer-operated backend;
- includes no advertising, user tracking, behavioral analytics, or third-party analytics SDKs;
- does not request access to the camera, photos, microphone, contacts, or location; and
- does not sell personal information or use App data for targeted advertising.

## 4. Copying, Sharing, and Third Parties

Calculation content is written to the system clipboard only when you tap Copy Results.

Calculation content is provided to another app only after you use the share feature and select a destination. The destination's own privacy policy governs its handling of that information.

Purchases, renewals, refunds, Family Sharing, restoration, and any optional diagnostics or App Analytics provided by Apple are processed independently by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We do not control data Apple collects independently. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for details.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

- Calculation history remains on your device until you delete records, choose Clear History, or uninstall the App.
- Preferences remain until changed, reset, or the App is uninstalled.
- Unsaved inputs are generally removed when the relevant view or App state resets.
- Purchase and subscription records retained by Apple are managed under Apple's policies. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings or the Apple Account subscription page in the App Store.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

You may choose not to save history, delete or clear local history, change preferences, stop using the App, or uninstall it at any time.

Because the current version stores no account or calculation data on a developer server, we generally hold no server-side personal data for you to access, correct, or delete. Contact us using the method in Section 10 if you have questions about the App's data practices.

## 7. Security

Local information is protected by the iOS app sandbox and device security features. No storage method can guarantee absolute security, so please protect your device and Apple Account.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose calculation tool and is not directed primarily to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If our data practices change, we will update this Policy and comply with applicable children's privacy requirements.

## 9. International Processing and Changes

We operate no server that receives App data and therefore do not actively transfer your calculation data across borders. Apple or a share destination you choose may process information in other countries or regions under its own privacy policy.

If the App's features or data practices materially change, we will update this Policy and its effective date and provide the revised version in the App or at the privacy policy URL listed on the App Store.

## 10. Contact Us

For questions about this Policy or the App's data practices, contact the developer using the App Support link on the App Store product page.
